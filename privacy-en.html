PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Yana is a mobile app of affirmations and motivational quotes: every day, lines chosen from a catalogue we write and review ourselves, to read in the app, in your reminders and in your widgets.

This policy explains what information Yana collects, why, who it is shared with and what your rights are. Yana is published by Stéphane Darryl Wamo Nkengue, doing business as Yana, in Granby (Québec, Canada) ("we").

In short: we keep only what is needed (your account, your favourites and followed topics, your subscription, and usage measurement without your text); no artificial intelligence processes your information; your answers to the welcome questionnaire stay on your phone; we sell nothing, run no ads and use no third-party tracking tools; you can delete everything from the app at any time.

1. WHAT WE COLLECT

In short: your account, your first name if you give it, your favourites and followed topics, your subscription, and usage measurement without your text. No location, no contacts, no biometric data.

What stays on your phone

This information is not sent to us: it is kept on your device and shared only with Yana's widgets on that same device.

  • Your answers to the welcome questionnaire: how you found Yana, your age range, your relationship status, your written goal, your goals, what motivates you, your practice, your view of manifestation. All these questions are optional.
  • Your settings: chosen tones, theme, reminder times and count, the quote pinned to your widget.
  • Your history (quotes you have already seen) and the content you have hidden.
  • "My photo": if you choose a photo as a background, it is copied to your phone only. Yana reads only the photo you select, never the rest of your library.

What you give us

  • Your first name, if you give it: it is used to greet you in the app.
  • Your favourites and followed topics: the identifiers of the quotes and topics you chose, so you find them again after reinstalling or on another device signed in to the same account.

Your account

  • Sign in with Apple: an identifier provided by Apple and, if you share it, your email address (which may be an Apple relay address).
  • Sign in with Google: your email address and name, provided by Google.
  • Without an account: a provisional account, with no email, linked to your device.

Your subscription

Your subscription status, plan and dates, provided by Apple and RevenueCat. We never see your payment information: it stays with Apple.

Usage measurement

To understand what helps and what gets in the way, we record events: welcome steps completed, app opens, quotes displayed, favourites, shares (the channel chosen, never the content sent), followed topics, the chosen theme, the number and size of widgets added, the subscription offer being shown, a purchase, the result of a sign-in. Each installation receives a random identifier, created by the app, that identifies neither you nor your device; it is linked to your account once the account exists. No text you write is part of this measurement. We use no third-party analytics or advertising tools, no advertising identifier, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Your device

  • Time zone and language, so that the tone of the quotes follows your day and the content is in your language.
  • Notifications: your reminders are scheduled on your device. No notification token is sent to us.
  • Photos: when you save a quote as an image, Yana adds it to your library without reading anything from it.
  • Technical logs from our servers (IP address, date, errors), kept briefly for security and troubleshooting.

2. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

  • To provide the service: choosing the quotes in your feed, widgets and reminders based on your topics, the time of day and your settings.
  • To keep your favourites and topics from one device or installation to another.
  • To manage your account and subscription, and to reserve content for subscribers after the trial.
  • To improve the app through the usage measurement described above.
  • To protect the service against abuse.

Choosing quotes is an automatic sort within a catalogue written in advance, the same for everyone who follows the same topics: no text is generated for you, and no automated decision has legal or similarly significant effects on you. We do not sell or rent your information, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not train any AI model with it.

Sharing a quote: when you send an image or text to another app (Messages, Instagram, WhatsApp…), it goes to that app, which uses it under its own rules. We only receive the channel chosen, for usage measurement.

4. HOW LONG WE KEEP YOUR INFORMATION

  • While your account exists: your first name, favourites, followed topics and subscription status.
  • Deleting your account: from Profile › Settings › Delete my account, your account and everything attached to it are deleted immediately and permanently. Usage events are detached from your account: only the random installation identifier remains, with no link to you.
  • On your phone: what is kept there (section 1) disappears when you delete the app.
  • Earlier versions of Yana: if you used Yana before November 2026 (journeys, audio sessions, discovery answers), that data remains attached to your account, unused, until it is deleted. Write to us to have it deleted without deleting your account.
  • With our providers: Apple and RevenueCat keep your purchase history under their own obligations. Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription: do that in your Apple ID settings.
  • Account without sign-in: it is linked to your device. If you uninstall the app without deleting it, write to us and we will delete it.

5. HOW WE PROTECT YOUR INFORMATION

Communication between the app and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS). Each account can access only its own data, through rules enforced in the database itself. The access keys to our services are never in the app. No measure is perfect, but we do what is needed to protect your information and will notify you, as the law requires, of any privacy incident presenting a serious risk.

6. MINIMUM AGE

Yana is for people 16 and older. We do not knowingly collect information about younger people. If you believe someone under 16 is using Yana, write to us at customersupport.app@gmail.com: we will delete their account.

7. YOUR RIGHTS AND HOW TO EXERCISE THEM

Depending on where you live (Québec, Canada, European Union, United Kingdom, Switzerland, certain US states), you may in particular:

  • access your information and get a copy, including in a structured, commonly used format;
  • have it corrected — your first name, favourites and topics can be changed directly in the app;
  • have it deleted — directly in Profile › Settings › Delete my account;
  • withdraw your consent (notifications, photo access) in your device settings;
  • object to usage measurement or ask for it to be restricted;
  • lodge a complaint with an authority: in Québec, the Commission d'accès à l'information; in Canada, the Office of the Privacy Commissioner; in the European Union, your country's data protection authority; in the United Kingdom, the ICO.

Legal bases (European Union, United Kingdom): performance of the service you ask for (your feed, favourites, subscription), your consent (notifications, photos) and our legitimate interest (usage measurement, security, abuse prevention).

US residents: we do not sell your personal information or share it for targeted advertising. You can exercise the rights your state's law gives you through the means below, and appeal a refusal by writing to us.

To exercise your rights: write to customersupport.app@gmail.com or use this form. We reply within 30 days and may ask you to confirm your identity.

8. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We will update this policy when the app or the law changes. The date at the top shows the latest version. For significant changes, we will let you know in the app.

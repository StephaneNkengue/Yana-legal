PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Yana is a mobile app that guides you toward a personal goal over a journey of 14 to 90 days, with two short audio sessions a day, written and read by an artificial intelligence from what you share with it.

This policy explains what information Yana collects, why, who it is shared with and what your rights are. Yana is published by Stéphane Darryl Wamo Nkengue, doing business as Yana, in Granby (Quebec, Canada) ("we").

Summary: we collect what is needed to write your sessions; your texts go through OpenAI, with your explicit agreement; we sell nothing, run no ads and use no third-party tracking tools; you can delete everything from the app at any time.

1. WHAT WE COLLECT

In short: what you write and choose in the app, your account, your subscription, and usage measurement that never includes your texts. No location, no contacts, no photos, no biometric data.

What you give us

  • Your profile: first name, age, gender (optional), how you are living your life right now, the voice and background sound you chose, your reminder times.
  • Your goal and its answers: the area you chose, the goal as you write it, and your answers to the discovery questions (your situation, your stage, why it matters, why now, what holds you back, what you tell yourself when it gets hard, what you have already tried, the scene you are aiming for, the time you have).
  • Your sessions: the mood given at check-in, whether the day's mission is done and why not, your evening "small win", and your feedback after listening (rating, tags such as "too long", free note).
  • What we produce for you: your journey plan, the texts and audio files of your sessions, your missions.

These texts are yours and may contain personal, even sensitive, information (your emotional state, health, work or financial situation). Only write what you are willing to share with Yana and its AI provider (section 3).

Your account

  • Sign in with Apple: an identifier provided by Apple and, if you share it, your email address (which may be an Apple relay address).
  • Sign in with Google: your email address and name, provided by Google.
  • Without an account: a provisional account, with no email, tied to your device.

Your subscription

Your subscription status, plan and dates, provided by Apple and RevenueCat. We never see your payment information: it stays with Apple.

Usage measurement

To understand where the app helps and where it gets in the way, we record events: the welcome and sign-up screens you go through, a session started, a reminder tapped and what it opened, the result of a sign-in. Each installation gets a random identifier, created by the app, that identifies neither you nor your device; it is linked to your account once created. None of the text you write goes into this measurement. We use no third-party analytics or advertising tools, no advertising identifier, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Your device

  • Time zone and language, so your morning and evening sessions follow your day.
  • Notification token, if you allow notifications. Your daily reminders are scheduled on your device.
  • DeviceCheck: an Apple service that lets us keep, at Apple, two bits tied to your device. We use it only so that a free offer cannot be claimed several times on the same device. It gives us no information about you.
  • Technical server logs (IP address, time, errors), kept briefly for security and troubleshooting.

Microphone and dictation

The microphone is used only when you tap the "dictate" button of a field, and only for the length of the dictation. Speech recognition is performed by Apple, on your device when possible, otherwise by Apple's servers. The sound is never recorded or sent to us: only the resulting text is written into the field, and it is then handled like typed text.

2. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

  • To provide the service: build your journey, write and read your morning and evening sessions, suggest your missions, schedule your reminders.
  • To adapt your sessions: your feedback (length, energy, relevance of missions) adjusts the following sessions of your journey.
  • To manage your account and subscription.
  • To protect the service: usage limits, prevention of free-offer abuse.
  • To improve the app through the usage measurement described above.
  • For your safety: if what you write suggests distress, the app does not write a session and shows you support resources. Yana is not a medical or emergency service.

We do not sell or rent your information, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not train any AI model with it. The content of your sessions is generated automatically, but no automated decision has a legal or similarly significant effect on you.

3. ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE: OPENAI

In short: your texts are sent to OpenAI to write your sessions and produce the voice. The app asks for your explicit agreement before the first transfer.

Yana uses OpenAI services (United States) for two things: writing the text of your journey and sessions, and turning it into speech.

What is sent to OpenAI: your first name, your gender if you gave it (to write grammatically correct French), the area and the goal, your answers to the discovery questions, your check-in (mood, mission done or not, small win), your recent feedback and notes, your journey plan and a summary of your latest sessions. Your age, email address and subscription information are not sent to it.

Your agreement: before the first transfer, the app explains what goes to OpenAI and asks for your explicit agreement. Without it, Yana cannot write your sessions. You can withdraw your agreement by deleting your account (section 8).

What OpenAI does with it: under its terms for developer services, OpenAI does not use this data to train its models and may keep it for up to 30 days to detect abuse before deleting it. Learn more: OpenAI's privacy policy.

AI-generated content can be imperfect. It supports you; it is not medical, psychological, financial or legal advice, nor a promise of results.

5. HOW LONG WE KEEP YOUR INFORMATION

  • As long as your account exists: your profile, your journeys (including completed or abandoned ones, which you can read again), your sessions and their audio files.
  • Account deletion: from Profile › Delete my account, your account, profile, journeys, sessions, audio files, feedback and notification token are deleted immediately and permanently. Usage-measurement events are detached from your account: only the random installation identifier remains, with no link to you.
  • At our providers: OpenAI keeps what was sent to it for up to 30 days (section 3). Apple and RevenueCat keep your purchase history under their own obligations. Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription: do that in your Apple ID settings.
  • Account without sign-in: it is tied to your device. If you uninstall the app without deleting it, write to us and we will delete it.

6. HOW WE PROTECT YOUR INFORMATION

Traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS). Each account can only access its own data, through rules enforced in the database itself. Audio files are private and can only be read through temporary links. The keys to the AI services are never in the app. No measure is perfect, but we do what is needed to protect your information and will notify you, as the law requires, of any privacy incident that presents a serious risk.

7. MINIMUM AGE

Yana is intended for people aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect information about younger people. If you believe someone under 16 is using Yana, write to digest.mtl@gmail.com and we will delete their account.

8. YOUR RIGHTS AND HOW TO EXERCISE THEM

Depending on where you live (Quebec, Canada, the European Union, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, some US states), you may in particular:

  • access your information and get a copy, including in a structured, commonly used format;
  • have it corrected — most of it can be edited directly in Profile;
  • have it deleted — directly in Profile › Delete my account;
  • withdraw your consent (to AI, microphone, notifications): permissions are withdrawn in your device settings; since AI processing is essential to the service, withdrawing it means deleting your account;
  • object to usage measurement or ask for it to be restricted;
  • lodge a complaint with an authority: in Quebec, the Commission d'accès à l'information; in Canada, the Office of the Privacy Commissioner; in the European Union, your country's data protection authority; in the United Kingdom, the ICO.

Legal bases (European Union, United Kingdom): performance of the service you request (your journey and sessions), your consent (AI processing, microphone, notifications) and our legitimate interest (usage measurement, security, abuse prevention).

US residents: we do not sell your personal information or share it for targeted advertising. You can exercise the rights your state law gives you through the means below, and appeal a refusal by writing to us.

To exercise your rights: write to digest.mtl@gmail.com or use this form. We reply within 30 days and may ask you to confirm your identity.
